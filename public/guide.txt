Boom Boom Tower
How to play

Getting started
1. Extract the ZIP and open Play.exe.
2. Choose a song from Songs. All ten original tracks include music.
3. Pick a difficulty and press Play.

Match each colored rocket as it reaches the line. Keep the matching button
pressed until a hold note ends. Consecutive hits build your combo.

Controller
Xbox button labels are shown here. You can also use the D-pad.
Yellow: Y or D-pad up. Red: B or D-pad right.
Green: A or D-pad down. Blue: X or D-pad left.
Golden Tower: right trigger or right bumper.
Black Hole: left trigger or left bumper. Pause: Start.
Follow the button hints at the bottom of the menus.

Keyboard
Yellow: Up arrow or W. Red: Right arrow or D.
Green: Down arrow or S. Blue: Left arrow or A.
Golden Tower: Space or left Shift. Black Hole: left Ctrl or B.
Pause: Esc. Change keyboard controls in Options.

Bonuses
Regular hits charge Golden Tower. Holds charge Black Hole.
Activate both together to multiply their bonuses.

Ways to play
Standard: play a song and aim for a better score.
Practice: slow the song down and play without failing.
Autoplay: watch the game play the song.
Endurance: repeat a song as it gets faster.
Playlists: put songs in the order you want to play them.

Adding music
The ten original tracks include music. Other songs need a recording from you.
Bring a recording you have permission to play. Use the same song version.
Choose a song, then Add audio to select a music file or paste a YouTube link.
Choose Edit audio to change or remove it later.
The audio panel provides search terms with a Copy button.
For YouTube, you need internet. Check that the music lines up before playing.

Adding songs
Choose Import to add songs from BeatSaver, a supported GitHub link, or a song pack.

Scores and progress
Progress is saved on your PC in the user folder beside the game.
Keep that folder when updating. Online leaderboards are not available in this playtest.

Help opening the game
Use a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC with an up-to-date graphics driver.
Make sure the ZIP is fully extracted before opening Play.exe.
If Windows reports a missing VCRUNTIME DLL, install Microsoft's VC++ x64 runtime:
https://aka.ms/vs/17/release/vc_redist.x64.exe
If YouTube playback asks for WebView2, get it from Microsoft:
https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
